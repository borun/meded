Critical Surgical Rule

Mandatory Surgical Safety Warning

Medical & Operative Disclaimer

For Surgical Training & Reference Only: This operative manual and ureter safety guide are intended exclusively for certified gynecologists, surgical residents, and operative trainees. Surgical steps, retroperitoneal dissection planes, anatomical variations, and emergency injury management protocols must be individualized based on intraoperative findings and patient pathology. Never execute irreversible cuts or pedicle ligations without clear visual or tactile verification of vital structures (ureters, bladder pillars, and pelvic vasculature).
