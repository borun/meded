Pedagogical Framework

How MedEd Accelerates Learning

Traditional webinar recordings are bloated with introductions, Q&As, and dead time. MedEd restructures open medical content into an engaging clinical learning environment.

1. Curated Precision Timestamps

Jump directly to critical segment starts and stop before transitions or housekeeping. Save 40–50% of watching time while capturing full clinical pearls.

2. Markdown-Powered Clinical Notes

Structured diagnostic criteria (e.g. WHO 6th Ed, Poseidon), dosage tables, and surgical guidelines authored in clean Markdown rendered side-by-side with videos.

3. Zero Lock-in & Offline Progress

100% client-side. Progress tracking is stored locally in your browser’s localStorage. No accounts, cookies, or tracking servers needed.

Submit a PR

Mandatory Notice

Medical & Clinical Practice Disclaimer

Strictly for Academic & Professional Training: The MedEd platform, its curated lecture modules, surgical manuals, and AI-assisted notes are designed exclusively for medical practitioners, postgraduate trainees, and students.

  • No Medical Advice: Content does NOT constitute individualized medical advice, diagnostic directives, or treatment orders.
  • Institutional Protocols Apply: Surgical techniques, drug regimens, and clinical algorithms must always be verified against current institutional guidelines and official society standards (e.g. ACOG, RCOG, FOGSI, ESHRE, WHO).
  • Patient-Specific Care: Individual patient factors, allergies, and contraindications always supersede generic educational materials.
